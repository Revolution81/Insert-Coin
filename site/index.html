A Bitcoin wallet for the M5Stack Cardputer Zero

Your keys, your coins, 16-bit.

Insert Coin turns a pocket Linux computer into a Bitcoin wallet that looks and sounds like a game from 1993. It starts with every radio off, and only the signer, in its own locked-down process, can ever touch your keys.

Choose mode

Two worlds. Vault is the default.

Every Insert Coin starts in Vault mode with Wi-Fi switched off. Go Online only when you want the convenience of a hot wallet, and switch back with one key.

VAULT

Radios off

  • Cold storage: the device never touches the internet.
  • Sign payments as PSBTs over animated QR codes or the microSD card.
  • Works with watch-only apps like Sparrow on your computer.

For savings you rarely move.

ONLINE

Wi-Fi and Tor

  • Check balances and send straight from the device.
  • Uses a public server through Tor, or your own node: just scan its QR.
  • An online cap limits what can leave each day.

For spending money. Keep the amount small.

How it keeps your keys safe

Four characters, and only one holds the keys.

Insert Coin is split into separate programs, each running as its own Linux user with only the permissions it needs; these four do the main work. If one part goes wrong, it still can't reach what the others guard.

SIR SIGNER czsigner

Holds the encrypted seed, asks for your PIN and your YES, and signs.

Can't reach the network at all.

CARDY czapp

Runs the menus and the watch-only wallet: balances, coins, history.

Never sees your PIN, seed words or private keys.

THE COURIER cznet

The only program on the internet: Wi-Fi, TLS, Tor and Electrum.

No keys, no screen, no wallet files.

THE LOOKOUT czscan

Runs the camera and reads QR codes in a sandbox.

No network, no files, no keys.

The signer draws its own screens. Look for the gold SIGNER shield and the security words you chose when you made the wallet. No shield, or the wrong words? Don't type your PIN.

Ten hearts, ten PIN tries

  • Insert Coin is brand new and hasn't been reviewed by outside security experts yet. Play with small amounts.
  • Ten wrong PINs erase the wallet. Your seed words are the only way back, so write them on paper or metal.
  • In Online mode it's a hot wallet. Keep savings on the savings edition in Vault mode, never on the spending app.
House rules

Your rules, enforced by the signer.

POLICY

Spending limits

Set a most-per-payment, a most-per-day, a cooldown between payments, a maximum fee rate and a list of the only addresses you'll pay. The signer checks every payment, offline or online.

PER TX · PER DAY · ALLOW-LIST
2FA

Phone codes

Link an authenticator app and big payments, or all of them, need a 6-digit code before the signer will sign.

TOTP · RFC 6238
BYPASS

Lock the rules

Add a bypass PIN and the rules can only loosen with both PINs. The seed words can't be shown or exported while it's set.

SECOND PIN · ARGON2ID
COUNTDOWN

Make them wait

After the right PIN, the wallet waits five minutes to a day before it opens. Anyone forcing you to unlock waits too.

5 MIN TO 24 H
WIPE PIN

One PIN to erase it all

Enter it and the device starts like new. Nothing is bricked; your seed words bring everything back. A duress PIN opens a decoy wallet instead.

WIPE · DURESS
AUTO LOCK

Locks when you walk away

Two to fifteen minutes, a time you choose, or never. Sharing files keeps it awake until you're done.

CHANGES NEED YOUR PIN
Power-ups

Small device. Full wallet.

SEEDS

Make it your own way

Every new seed mixes the system's randomness, the secure chip if fitted, and any dice rolls, coin flips, shuffled cards or key mashing you add. One weak source can't make a weak seed.

BIP39 · 12 or 24 words
BACKUP

Back up three ways

Write the words, show a SeedQR, or split the seed into shares so no single paper reveals it.

SEEDQR · SLIP39
ACCOUNTS

SegWit and Taproot

Several accounts per seed, child seeds for other wallets, and message signing.

BIP84 · BIP86 · BIP85 · BIP322
MULTISIG

Share the keys

Join a multisig as a cosigner, import descriptors, or set up an inheritance wallet with a time-locked heir key.

BIP48 · DESCRIPTORS
NODE

Bring your own node

Scan the server QR on your Umbrel or Start9 and the wallet talks only to your node. The Wi-Fi icon turns green on your own server, blue on the public one.

ELECTRUM · UMBREL · START9
QR

Sign with the camera

Scan a payment request, sign it and show the result as an animated QR code.

PSBT · UR · BBQR
COINS

Pick your coins

Choose exactly which coins a payment spends, merge small coins into one while fees are low, or send everything with the fee taken out of the amount.

COIN CONTROL · RBF
NFC

Tap a card

Read a SATSCARD, check it's genuine, and sweep its coins into your wallet. TAPSIGNER works too.

SATSCARD · TAPSIGNER
PAPER

Paper wallets

Make a fresh paper wallet with QR codes and print it at home over encrypted Wi-Fi, on any AirPrint or IPP Everywhere printer, no driver needed. VIEW ALL lists the ones you've made. The keys stay inside the device, never on the microSD card.

IPPS · PINNED PRINTER
SHARE

Wi-Fi file sharing

Grab your transaction history and paper wallets from a browser on your home network. Password-protected, download only, and it switches itself off. Set your own password before sharing paper wallets. Your browser warns that the page isn't private, because the wallet signs its own certificate: check the fingerprint matches the screen, then go ahead.

HTTPS · LOCAL NETWORK ONLY
ADD-ONS

Extra armor

Add a secure chip to bind the PIN to the hardware, or a fingerprint sensor for quick unlocks.

SECURE CHIP · FINGERPRINT
SETUP

Make it yours

Nine box colors or your own, screen sleep from two minutes to never, one switch for all sound, and the volume keys (Fn+D, Fn+S, Fn+A) with a bar on screen.

Spec sheet

Under the cartridge

WALLET
Seeds
BIP39, 12 or 24 words, encrypted at rest with Argon2id and AES-GCM
Addresses
Native SegWit (BIP84) and Taproot (BIP86)
Signing
PSBT by QR (UR, BBQr) or microSD; messages by BIP137 and BIP322
Multisig
BIP48 cosigner keys, output descriptors, inheritance policies
Backups
Seed words, SeedQR, SLIP39 split shares, encrypted card backup
Network
Electrum protocol over TLS and Tor, or your own node (Umbrel, Start9) by scanning its QR
Guards
Wipe PIN, duress PIN, login countdown, auto lock, spending policy with TOTP 2FA and bypass PIN
Updates
Signed (Ed25519) and installed from the card with SETUP › UPDATE, no download on the device

Get Insert Coin

SAVINGS EDITION

The wallet image

FOR SAVINGS
  • Burn it to a microSD card of 8 GB or more. The device boots into Insert Coin and runs nothing else.
  • No other apps on the device, so nothing else can get near your keys.
  • Built on M5Stack's November 2026 CardputerZero OS. Flip the power switch any time: it locks the card read-only within half a second.
  • Says SAVINGS WALLET on the title screen.
SPENDING EDITION

An app in M5Stack's launcher

FOR SPENDING MONEY ONLY
  • Keeps the CardputerZero home screen and your other apps; open Insert Coin Spending when you need it.
  • Apps you install from M5Stack's store could reach the wallet while it's open. Treat it like cash in your pocket.
  • Says SPENDING WALLET on the title screen.
